Job Title: Senior Data Scientist / NLP Engineer

Company: TechCorp AI

About the Role:
We are looking for a Senior Data Scientist with strong NLP expertise to join
our AI team. You will build and deploy machine learning models that process
and understand human language at scale.

Responsibilities:
- Design and implement NLP pipelines for text classification, NER, and
  information extraction
- Fine-tune and deploy large language models (BERT, GPT) for production use
- Build end-to-end ML pipelines from data ingestion to model serving
- Collaborate with product and engineering teams

Requirements:
- 4+ years of experience in data science or machine learning
- Strong Python skills (PyTorch, scikit-learn, Hugging Face Transformers)
- Experience with NLP techniques: NER, text classification, sentence embeddings
- Familiarity with spaCy, NLTK, or similar NLP libraries
- Experience deploying models with Docker and cloud platforms (AWS/GCP)
- Solid understanding of statistics and machine learning fundamentals

Nice to Have:
- Experience with LLMs, RAG, or LangChain
- Publications or open-source contributions in NLP
- Experience with MLflow or similar MLOps tools
